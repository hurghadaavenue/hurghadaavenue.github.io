Orange Bay – Hurghada

Orange Bay in Hurghada is one of the most beautiful marine destinations in the Red Sea, often called the “Maldives of Egypt” thanks to its turquoise waters, white sandy beaches, and integrated tourist facilities that make it a perfect spot for day trips and relaxation. It is located on Giftun Island and can only be reached by boat from Hurghada Marina.

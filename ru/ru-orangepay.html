Orange Bay – Hurghada

          Orange Bay in Hurghada is one of the most beautiful marine destinations in the Red Sea, often called the “Maldives of Egypt” thanks to its turquoise waters, white sandy beaches, and integrated tourist facilities that make it a perfect spot for day trips and relaxation. It is located on Giftun Island and can only be reached by boat from Hurghada Marina.

 

Natural Features

  • Long stretches of soft white sandy beaches
  • Crystal-clear turquoise waters ideal for swimming and photography
  • Diverse coral reefs for snorkeling and diving enthusiasts
  • The famous Sandbar, a shallow area where visitors can walk far into the sea — the island’s most iconic photo spot.

 

Services & Facilities

  • Beachfront restaurants and cafés serving seafood and refreshing drinks.
  • Sunbeds and umbrellas for relaxation.
  • A wooden walkway extending over shallow waters.
  • Simple beach huts for visitors.
  • Safe swimming zones with lifeguards.

 

Basic facilities 

  • restrooms.
  • freshwater showers.
  • diving center.

 

Activities

  • Snorkeling and diving trips to explore coral reefs and colorful fish.
  • Water sports such as kayaking and stand-up paddleboarding (SUP).
  • Photography sessions in stunning natural settings.
  • Organized daily tours including boat transfer, lunch, and snorkeling equipment.

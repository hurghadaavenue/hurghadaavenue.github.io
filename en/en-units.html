The Arab Contractors Excellence

The Arab Contractors Company—a leading Egyptian international government company backed by its extensive local and international experience, reliability, guarantee, and quality—uniquely presents the Avenue Compound in Hurghada, Red Sea, Egypt. This project is equally exceptional in Hurghada and the Red Sea due to its unmatched location, high-quality units, services, and facilities. Situated directly in front of the Hurghada International Airport entrance in Al-Kawthar, Hurghada’s premier residential and tourist district, the compound is just one minute away from Hurghada’s International Touristic Promenade.

Units are delivered with ultra-deluxe finishing adhering to Arab Contractors' high standards, available in four distinct layouts ranging from studios, one-bedroom apartments, and two-bedroom apartments to three-bedroom apartments, with areas ranging from 40m² up to 145m². The compound features an exclusive advantage: a basement garage dedicated to unit owners that spans the entire footprint of the compound—a massive 40,000m²—along with five swimming pools and vast, widespread landscaped greenery throughout the property. This provides units with distinctive views of the pools, gardens, and main streets, as the compound faces wide streets on all four sides with no adjacent or attached buildings.

The compound boasts a health club, a gym, sauna, and Jacuzzi facilities, an exclusive residents-only restaurant, an integrated commercial mall, a kids' club, and all other essential commercial services. Commercial spaces and amenities occupy a total area of 18,000m². This is further complemented by Al-Kawthar district’s abundance of social and commercial facilities, including hospitals, schools, shopping markets, restaurants, cafes, hotels, and international brand names.

All of the above is paired with a distinctive selection of flexible payment plans featuring special discounts and installment options extending up to 8 years, supported by secure, officially registered, and guaranteed property ownership contracts from the Arab Contractors, complete with a proportional share in the project's land and an absolute commitment to quality standards and delivery schedules. This makes the project the ultimate and most trusted choice for living, investment, and commerce in Hurghada, Red Sea. The project clearly highlights the unmatched excellence of the Arab Contractors in the quality of the structures, services, and facilities it builds—because distinction is always the hallmark of "The Arab Contractors."

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions and Answers

What apartment sizes are available at Avenue Compound Hurghada?

Apartments range from 40 m² to 145 m². Available layouts include studios and apartments with one, two or three bedrooms.

What installment plans are available?

Flexible payment plans with special discounts and installments of up to 8 years are available.

Where is Avenue Compound located?

The compound is in Al-Kawthar, Hurghada, directly opposite the entrance to Hurghada International Airport and about one minute from the International Touristic Promenade.

What facilities are available in the compound?

Facilities include five swimming pools, a Health Club with a gym, sauna and Jacuzzi, a residents-only restaurant, an integrated commercial mall, a kids' club, an owners' basement garage and landscaped greenery.

How are the apartments finished?

Apartments are delivered with ultra-deluxe finishing to Arab Contractors' standards. Ownership contracts are officially registered and include a proportional share in the project land.

Is there parking for owners?

Yes. A basement garage dedicated to unit owners spans the entire 40,000 m² footprint of the compound.

Are the ownership contracts registered?

Yes. Contracts from Arab Contractors are officially registered and guaranteed, and include a proportional share in the project’s land.

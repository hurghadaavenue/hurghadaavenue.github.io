              Hurghada Avenue is one of the most prominent integrated residential and commercial projects in the heart of Hurghada. It combines a strategic location in El Kawther District, comprehensive internal and external services, and modern design, making it an ideal choice for living and investment.

Location El Kawther District: The most organized and vibrant area in Hurghada.

 

Close to Hurghada Promenade: Just steps away from international restaurants, cafés, and shops.

 

Near Hurghada International Airport: Only 1–3 km away, perfect for frequent travelers and tourism investment.

 

Access to public and private beaches nearby for marine activities.

 

Logistics & Accessibility

  • Excellent connectivity: Linked to main roads (Resorts Road & El Kawther Main Street), with easy access to Downtown (Dahar), El Gouna to the north, and Sahl Hasheesh & Makadi to the south.
  • Transportation: Taxis and modern ride services (Uber, Indrive) available 24/7.

 

Surrounding Services & Facilities

  • Healthcare: Close to El Masry Hospital, El Nile Hospital, and El Ezaby Pharmacy.
  • Shopping: Surrounded by Carrefour, Fathallah, and Russian Market.
  • Banks & ATMs: Major banks have branches in the district.
  • Education: International schools and nurseries serving residents and expats.

 

Compound Features

  • Trusted Developer: Arab Contractors, with over 70 years of experience.
  • Integrated Project: Residential units (studios to 3-bedroom apartments) + 69 commercial units.

 

Internal Facilities

  • 5 swimming pools
  • Internal promenade with shops, restaurants, and cafés
  • 14,000 m² of landscaped green areas
  • Children’s zones & celebration areas
  • 24/7 security with electronic gates & CCTV
  • Underground parking & modern elevators

 

Investment & Tourism Advantages

  • High rental yield: 5–7% for long-term rentals, up to 16% for short-term holiday rentals.
  • Growing property value: Located in a fully serviced district ensuring continuous appreciation.
  • Unit variety: 35% studios, 20% one-bedroom, 25% two-bedroom, 20% three-bedroom apartments.

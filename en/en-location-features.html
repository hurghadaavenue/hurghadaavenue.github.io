Frequently asked questions

Questions & Answers

Where is Avenue Compound located?

Avenue Compound Hurghada is located in El Kawther, the city’s premier residential and touristic district, directly opposite the entrance of Hurghada Airport.

How far is the compound from Hurghada International Airport?

The compound is only 1–3 km from Hurghada International Airport, which suits frequent travelers and tourism investment.

What is near the compound?

The compound is close to Hurghada Promenade with international restaurants, cafés and shops, to El Masry Hospital, El Nile Hospital and El Ezaby Pharmacy, to Carrefour, Fathallah and the Russian Market, and to banks, ATMs, and international schools and nurseries.

How do I get around from the compound?

The compound is linked to Resorts Road and El Kawther Main Street, with easy access to Downtown (Dahar), El Gouna to the north, and Sahl Hasheesh and Makadi to the south. Taxis and ride services such as Uber and Indrive are available 24/7.

What facilities are inside the compound?

The compound has 5 swimming pools, an internal promenade with shops, restaurants and cafés, 14,000 m² of landscaped green areas, children’s zones and celebration areas, 24/7 security with electronic gates and CCTV, and underground parking with modern elevators.

What types of units are available?

Residential units range from studios to three-bedroom apartments, plus 69 commercial units. The mix is 35% studios, 20% one-bedroom, 25% two-bedroom and 20% three-bedroom apartments.

The Arab Contractors Excellence

The Arab Contractors Company—an international Egyptian government-backed entity known for reliability, guarantees, and quality—introduces Avenue Compound in Hurghada, Red Sea. Its location in Al-Kawthar places it directly in front of Hurghada International Airport entrance, just about one minute from the International Touristic Promenade.

Units are delivered with ultra-deluxe finishing to Arab Contractors’ high standards. Available layouts include studios, one-bedroom apartments, two-bedroom apartments, and three-bedroom apartments, with areas from 40 m² to 145 m².

A major advantage is the owners’ basement garage spanning the entire 40,000 m² compound footprint—paired with five swimming pools and vast landscaped greenery. The compound faces wide streets on all four sides with no adjacent attached buildings, providing open views of pools, gardens, and main streets.

Residents enjoy a Health Club (gym, sauna and Jacuzzi), a residents-only restaurant, an integrated commercial mall, kids’ club, and essential commercial services. Commercial spaces cover a total area of 18,000 m². The Al-Kawthar district also offers hospitals, schools, shopping markets, restaurants, cafes, hotels, and well-known international brand names.

Flexible payment plans include special discounts and installment options up to 8 years. Ownership contracts are officially registered, secure, and guaranteed, including a proportional share in the project land—fully aligned with delivery schedules and quality standards.

Arab Contractors · Avenue Compound

A Compound Designed Around Comfort

Avenue Compound in Hurghada is built by Arab Contractors, a state-owned Egyptian company with international experience, and is unique in its location, apartment quality, finishing and infrastructure. It stands directly opposite the entrance of Hurghada International Airport, in El Kawther, the city’s first tourist residential district, and only one minute from the international tourist promenade.

The exclusive underground garage for owners occupies the whole basement area, which matches the 40,000 m² footprint of the compound. Above it you will find five swimming pools and wide green areas, giving apartments open views of pools, gardens and main roads. The compound is surrounded by wide roads on all four sides, with no adjacent buildings.

Residents enjoy a health club with a gym, sauna and jacuzzi, a restaurant for owners, a shopping mall and a kids club. Commercial and service areas cover 18,000 m², and El Kawther adds hospitals, schools, markets, restaurants, cafés, hotels and international brands nearby.

Apartments are delivered in Ultra-Deluxe finishing across four types, from studios to three bedrooms, ranging from 40 m² to 145 m². Flexible payment plans with attractive discounts and installments of up to 8 years make the project a reliable choice for living, investing and business in Hurghada on the Red Sea.

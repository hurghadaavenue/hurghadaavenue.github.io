Orange Bay – Hurghada

Orange Bay in Hurghada is one of the most beautiful marine destinations in the Red Sea, often called the “Maldives of Egypt” thanks to its turquoise waters, white sandy beaches, and integrated tourist facilities that make it a perfect spot for day trips and relaxation. It is located on Giftun Island and can only be reached by boat from Hurghada Marina.

Frequently asked questions

Questions & Answers

What is Orange Bay?

Orange Bay is one of the most beautiful marine destinations in the Red Sea, often called the “Maldives of Egypt” thanks to its turquoise waters, white sandy beaches and integrated tourist facilities.

Who gets the free membership?

Unit owners in Avenue Compound by Arab Contractors in Hurghada receive a free membership at Orange Bay.

How do I get to Orange Bay?

Orange Bay is located on Giftun Island and can only be reached by boat from Hurghada Marina.

What services are available on the island?

There are beachfront restaurants and cafés serving seafood and refreshing drinks, sunbeds and umbrellas, a wooden walkway over shallow waters, simple beach huts, and safe swimming zones with lifeguards on duty. Restrooms, freshwater showers and a diving center are also available.

What activities can I do there?

You can go snorkeling and diving to explore coral reefs, try kayaking and stand-up paddleboarding (SUP), take photography sessions, or join organized daily tours that include boat transfer, lunch and snorkeling equipment.

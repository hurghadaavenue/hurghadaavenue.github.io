Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Prices and Installments

How much does an apartment cost at Avenue Compound Hurghada?

Cash prices start from 1,800,000 EGP for a 40 m² studio and go up to 6,525,000 EGP for a 145 m² three-bedroom unit, at 45,000 EGP per m² for cash. With installments up to 8 years, the price is calculated at 69,000 EGP per m².

What payment plans are available?

Cash with a 35% discount, or installments over 1 year with a 25% discount, 2 years 20%, 3 years 15%, 4 years 10%, 5 years 5%, and no discount for 6, 7 and 8 years.

How much is the down payment?

The down payment is 20% for installments up to 6 years, 30% for 7 years and 40% for 8 years. Cash is paid in full at signing.

Is there interest on installments?

No. Installments up to 8 years are interest-free, and can be paid yearly, quarterly or monthly.

What is the maintenance deposit?

The maintenance deposit is 10% of the price before discount and is paid in addition to the unit price.

When is delivery?

Delivery is in June 2028, and the units come with Tradi-Luxe finishing from Arab Contractors.

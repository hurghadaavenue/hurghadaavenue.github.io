Who we are

Arab Contractors

Building the Future of the Middle East and Africa

Arab Contractors is a leading force in the construction sector across the Middle East and Africa, with a diverse project portfolio spanning more than 29 countries. With over 70 years of expertise, the company’s success is built on collaboration with clients, partners, and suppliers, and a steadfast commitment to excellence.

Building the Future

Arab Contractors continues to push boundaries by adopting sustainable practices and innovative technologies such as Building Information Modeling (BIM), helping shape the future of the construction industry. Its commitment to social responsibility extends to employee welfare and community development, leaving a positive and lasting impact beyond its projects. In short, Arab Contractors is not just a construction company, but a driving force for progress, development, and cooperation—building the future of the Middle East and Africa, project by project.

Founded as an Egyptian joint-stock company on 22/11/1975 by Ministerial Decree No. 199 of 1975, MIC is currently governed by Law No. 159 of 1981 on joint-stock companies, partnerships limited by shares, and limited liability companies, as well as Law No. 95 of 1992 on the Capital Market and Law No. 8 of 1997 on Investment Guarantees and Incentives.

Arab Contractors owns 97.86% of MIC’s capital. The company is registered with the Egyptian Federation for Construction and Building Contractors under membership No. 559, classified in road and bridge construction and maintenance, building and finishing works, restoration of antiquities, water and sewage networks, and general works.

MIC’s team, spread across more than 14 governorates in Egypt, works together to achieve the highest performance standards. The company is committed to delivering high-quality services and products, firmly believing that its growth is tied to its human resources. Its history demonstrates expertise across all engineering fields. With a core mission of keeping pace with globalization, MIC strives to ensure customer satisfaction.

Studio Apartment 50 m²

A Compact Home in Hurghada’s Prime District

This 50 m² studio combines a reception area, a bathroom and a terrace in a single, efficient layout, delivered with Ultra-Deluxe finishing by Arab Contractors, a state-owned Egyptian company with more than 70 years of experience.

Avenue Compound stands in El Kawther, the city’s first tourist residential district, directly opposite the entrance of Hurghada International Airport. The compound is surrounded by wide roads on all four sides with no adjacent buildings, so units enjoy open views over pools, gardens and main streets.

Ownership contracts are officially registered and include a proportional share in the project land.
